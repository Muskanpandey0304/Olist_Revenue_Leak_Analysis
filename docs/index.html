Late delivery hurts reviews, not repeat purchase. Customers whose first order was late came back at 2.03% against 2.31% (not significant, p = 0.15). About 93% of the extra time in late orders is carrier transit.

States with higher income buy far more per resident. Income explains 62% of the variation (SP excluded), and sales are lowest where delivery is slowest and freight dearest. Paraná is the proposed first test.
